Brand Essence

Our Mission

Ball Run transforms everyday streets into places of discovery, encouraging people of all ages to explore, connect and play within their city.

Inspired by the spirit of outdoor adventure, Ball Run brings moments of curiosity into urban environments through beautifully crafted public marble runs.

---

Identity

The ball run identity draws inspiration from:

- Vintage camping badges
- National Park signage
- Heritage wayfinding
- Scout patches
- Outdoor equipment branding
- Screen printed posters

---

Flag Logo

The flag logo is designed for flexible applications.
Use for: City branding, Event banners, Wayfinding, Apparel, Digital assets

---

Colour Palette

The palette reflects the outdoors while introducing a vibrant accent inspired by playful urban discovery.

Warm Stone: #ece8dc (Primary background)
Trail Orange: #e84b15 (Primary accent)
Forest Green: #4a6242 (Secondary accent)
Charcoal Navy: #192c32 (Primary text & dark backgrounds)

The orange should be used intentionally to highlight calls to
action, key information and playful moments.

Typography


Display: New Spirit
Usage: Hero headings, feature titles, event names
Style: Title Case, Regular, generous spacing, large scale, never all uppercase

Heading: Barlow Condensed
Used for: Navigation, Section headings, Buttons, Labels, Maps, Signage
Style: Uppercase, Medium or Semibold, increased letter spacing

Body: Baskerville
Used for: Paragraphs, articles, descriptions, editorial content
