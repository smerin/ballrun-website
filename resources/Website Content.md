## HOME

### Hero

**Urban Play, Re-Imagined**

Hand-built installations, brought to the heart of towns and cities. Come and play — no ticket, no timer, just balls, runs, and a bit of wonder.

### Intro

The Ball Run brings hand-built installations to public spaces in towns and cities. Load your ball, set it moving, and watch it wind, tip, dip and race its way through — then keep it as a souvenir and come back as many times as you want.

Ball Runs are designed to get people off their phones and outside — bringing families, friends and strangers together through hands-on play.

Find us from 3rd August: The Great Bristol Ball Run — Anchor Square & Millennium Square. Buy sustainable wooden balls from the We The Curious shop.

And this is just the start — more ball runs, more spaces, more play, more towns and cities to come.

## THE GREAT BRISTOL BALL RUN

Six runs — five weaving through Anchor Square and Millennium Square—each built differently, with unique challenges and ways to play and explore.

Race a friend through a labyrinth of spinning discs by the Aquarium. Send balls flying through a skittle run near the harbourside. Balance your way along a tilting, wavy channel, or steer through a hands-on tabletop maze nearby. Beside the fountains on Millennium Square, take on our longest city run — watch a ball climb an Archimedes screw lift before setting off a overhead wire track.

Every run on the trail is built to surprise and encourage play and curiosity.

Get your balls from We The Curious shop and use as often as you want.

This pop-up is just the beginning. We want to make Bristol the UK's first truly playable city — with Ball Run installations woven into daily life right across it — and then take that same ambition to towns and cities across the country.

## ABOUT

### Who we are

The Ball Run is brought to you by Oddball CIC — a social enterprise on a mission to get more play into everyday town and city life. We design, build, and install play installations wherever there's room for them — squares, parks, streets, beachfronts, and everywhere in between — so play becomes part of everyday life, not something you have to seek out.

### The craft

Every run is designed and hand-built by our team, led by Simon Jackman, who has spent more than 20 years building sustainable playgrounds and play equipment, John Packer and Ruby Myers. Each installation is built to last — built to handle the weather, the crowds, and years of play, wherever it ends up.

### The balls

Our balls are turned from sustainable, FSC certified timber, made in our solar-powered workshop in Somerset. Buy one, keep it as a souvenir, and come back as often as you like.

### Proven ground

Before Ball Run brought this experience to the city, our sister organisations, Green Play Project and The Wooden Ball Company, proved it in the wild. Their installations at the Eden Project and Forestry England sites, including Alice Holt Forest, have been a firm favourite with visitors of all ages — and now we're bringing that same experience to towns and cities.
